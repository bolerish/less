Space to think

A little room
to think.

Small pauses, open windows, and the quiet pleasure of doing one thing at a time.

Fieldnotes editorial · 6 min read

There is a kind of clarity that arrives when we stop adding. A desk with room for a notebook. A walk without a destination. A page with space around the words.

Attention is a small, everyday practice. Sometimes the most useful change is simply making a little room.
